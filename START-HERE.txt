PITWALL ENGINEER 0.1.2 — LMU RACE OPERATIONS

ENGINEER
1. Extract this whole folder. Double-click lmu-engineer.exe.
2. The dashboard opens at http://127.0.0.1:7437.
3. Open Setup & event rules > Drivers.
4. Enter your reachable public hostname/IP:7443, or private VPN address:7443.
5. Export one shared sender package per car. All co-drivers of that car use the same EXE; each PC creates its own identity automatically.
6. Send the shared package to that car's drivers. The pairing credential is private.
7. Forward TCP 7443 to this PC, or connect your team over a private network. Keep HTTP 7437 local.
8. Check event assumptions and service times before making strategy calls.
9. Use the receiver tray menu to reopen the dashboard or stop it after the race.

DRIVER
Extract the paired package and double-click its lmu-agent.exe before driving. Enable Plugins in LMU gameplay settings. Keep the agent running across your stint. It reconnects and records during outages. Right-click its tray icon for status or Stop.

IMPORTANT
A stream alone cannot provide fuel, pedal, tyre or energy telemetry. The agent must run on each driving PC. Spectator data is labelled scoring-only.

Read README-ENGINEER.md for connection troubleshooting, strategy assumptions, imports and recovery. ENGINEER-VALIDATION.md records the completed checks and deployment scope.

The app uses local game telemetry. It does not sign in to Steam or RaceControl, fetch ratings/results history, change game controls, or send driver instructions automatically.
